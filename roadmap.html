How this tracker works

for either of us, or any Claude session
  1. Read it in three seconds. The Overview headline says which week we're in, how far the pilot is, and whether we're on track. Green means on schedule; amber means one week behind; red means more.
  2. Tick things off here. Check tasks or change a week's status. Edits stay in this browser and show as "unsaved changes" until they're exported.
  3. Export to make it permanent. Export copies the updated data and downloads it. Luke Kerner (or a Claude session) drops it into the file and commits — git history is the audit trail.
  4. Or just tell a Claude session. "Mark week 3 done," "slip week 5 by a week" — the editing rules are written inside this file, so any session knows what to change.
  5. Slips get recorded, not erased. A slipped week is marked and explained. The tracker tells the truth about how the build actually went.
